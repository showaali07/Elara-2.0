medical_information Emergency First Aid Quick Guides

❤️ Chest Pain / Suspected Heart Attack

Keep patient seated and calm. Loosen tight clothing around neck and waist. Do not offer food or heavy water. Administer Sorbitrate/Aspirin only if pre-prescribed by doctor.

🩸 Heavy Bleeding / Trauma

Apply firm continuous pressure directly on wound using clean cloth or sterile gauze. Keep affected limb elevated above heart level if possible.

🐍 Snake Bite Protocol

Immobilize the bitten limb with a splint. Keep bite site at or below heart level. DO NOT apply tight tourniquets or cut the wound. Transport to PHC immediately for ASV.

☀️ Heat Stroke & Dehydration

Move patient to cool shaded spot. Fan air and sponge skin with cool water. Sip Jan Aushadhi WHO-standard ORS solution slowly.
